#4471 is three problems

owner support desk draft reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

#4471 went out as one known issue with one fix queued. Read by tenant and by what the reporter saw, its reports are three different bugs. One fix closes one of them.

Why they were merged

Triage merges on the subject line (decision 0001). A subject like “Wrong day on the invite” reads the same whatever broke the date, so reports of all three landed in one ticket and nobody had to read a body to put them there.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant here writes the day first. Day and month swap. Fixed by parsing in the tenant’s locale order. This is the fix already queued for #4471.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type MM/DD/YYYY, they do, and the form reads it day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixed by printing the hint in the tenant’s order; the parser fix alone leaves it broken.
  • timezone: the date moves by one day instead of swapping, depending on where the attendee is. The desk noted these as one-day moves. Fixed by handling time zones when the invite is built; neither fix above touches it.
Count by tenant and by what the reporter saw, not by the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter's browser, and its guess disagrees with the tenant often enough to move reports from one problem to another.

Lesson

A matching subject says the symptom is the same, not the bug. Before a big ticket is called one issue, split its reports by cause and check the split adds up.
